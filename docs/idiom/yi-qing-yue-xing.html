怡情悦性

yí qíng yuè xìng
Literal: "joy, mood, delight, nature"
Home › Emotion › 怡情悦性

📖 Meaning

To cheer the heart and soothe the disposition. It describes something — such as music, scenery, art, or literature — that brings mental ease and emotional pleasure, lifting one's spirits and calming the mind.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom first appears in Chapter 17 of Cao Xueqin's Qing-dynasty masterpiece 'Dream of the Red Chamber' (《红楼梦》). The character Jia Zheng, a Confucian official weighed down by administrative duties, laments that as he has grown older, he has become unfamiliar with writings that '怡情悦性' — those that delight the heart and nourish the spirit. The phrase captures the classical Chinese ideal of cultivating inner harmony through aesthetic and literary pursuits.

💡 Example Usage

闲暇时听听古琴曲，最能怡情悦性。

Listening to guqin music in one's spare time is the best way to soothe the mind and lift the spirits.
