表面文章

biǎo miàn wén zhāng
Literal: "surface, face, writing, chapter"
Home › Quality › 表面文章

📖 Meaning

This idiom describes work or behavior that is superficially impressive but lacks substance or genuine effort. It refers to a perfunctory,敷衍 approach where one goes through the motions, focusing on appearances rather than real results or practical outcomes.

📜 Origin Story

No specific classical origin is recorded for this modern idiom. It emerged from colloquial Chinese usage to criticize bureaucratic formalism and hollow posturing. The phrase gained prominence in political discourse, most notably when Deng Xiaoping used it in his 1978 speech at the National Science Conference to condemn formalism that prioritizes superficial display over actual efficiency, speed, quality, and cost-effectiveness.

💡 Example Usage

他的工作报告全是表面文章，没有解决任何实际问题。

His work report was nothing but superficial window-dressing, solving no real problems at all.
