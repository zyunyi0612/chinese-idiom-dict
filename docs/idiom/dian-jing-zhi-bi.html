点睛之笔

diǎn jīng zhī bǐ
Literal: "dot, eye, of, brush-stroke"
Home › Quality › 点睛之笔

📖 Meaning

The brushstroke that dots the eyes — the finishing touch that brings a work of art or writing to life. It denotes the most vivid, inspired, and masterful passage in a piece of writing or artwork, the line that gives the whole its soul.

📜 Origin Story

Recorded in the Book of Jin (晋书), in the Biography of Gu Kaizhi, a celebrated painter of the Eastern Jin dynasty. Gu Kaizhi would often finish a figure painting yet leave the eyes unmarked for years. When asked why, he replied that the beauty or ugliness of the four limbs was not where the real wonder lay; conveying the spirit and true likeness depended entirely on the eyes. The anecdote gave rise to the metaphor of the eye-dotting stroke as the crucial, life-giving touch in any work.

💡 Example Usage

这段景物描写是全篇的点睛之笔，读后令人回味无穷。

This passage of scenery is the crowning touch of the whole essay, lingering in the mind long after reading.
