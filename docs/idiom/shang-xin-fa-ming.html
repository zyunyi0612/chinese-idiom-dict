赏信罚明

shǎng xìn fá míng
Literal: "reward, trust, punish, clear"
Home › Leadership › 赏信罚明

📖 Meaning

To handle affairs strictly and fairly, rewarding those who are trustworthy and punishing wrongdoers clearly. It is synonymous with '赏罚分明' (rewards and punishments are clearly defined). The idiom emphasizes that fair and consistent rewards and punishments are essential to maintaining order and authority.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom is a variant of the more common '赏罚分明' (rewards and punishments are clearly defined). The concept of fair reward and punishment has deep roots in ancient Chinese political philosophy, particularly in the Legalist (法家) tradition advocated by thinkers like Han Fei Zi and Shang Yang. They argued that a ruler must apply rewards and punishments consistently and transparently to govern effectively. The specific variant '赏信罚明' emphasizes that rewards should be based on trustworthiness and punishments should be clear and unambiguous.

💡 Example Usage

只有赏信罚明，才能让团队心服口服。

Only by rewarding the trustworthy and punishing clearly can one earn the team's wholehearted respect.
