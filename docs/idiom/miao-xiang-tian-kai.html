妙想天开

miào xiǎng tiān kāi
Literal: "wonderful, think, heaven, open"
Home › Wisdom › 妙想天开

📖 Meaning

Describes having bizarre, whimsical, or fanciful ideas that are detached from reality. It conveys imagination that borders on the absurd or impractical. The phrase is closely related to the more common idiom 异想天开.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom is a variant form of the well-known expression 异想天开 (yì xiǎng tiān kāi), which dates back to vernacular Chinese fiction of the Qing dynasty. The phrase combines 妙想 (ingenious or fanciful thoughts) with 天开 (heaven opening apart), painting a picture of ideas so extraordinary they seem to split the sky. No single classical text is definitively credited as the origin of this particular variant. It has been used colloquially in modern Chinese to characterize unrealistic or overly creative thinking.

💡 Example Usage

他总是妙想天开，觉得不努力也能一夜暴富。

He always has wild fantasies, believing he can strike it rich overnight without any effort.
