妙算神机

miào suàn shén jī
Literal: "marvelous, calculation, divine, mechanism"
Home › Wisdom › 妙算神机

📖 Meaning

This idiom describes someone with boundless resourcefulness who can keenly assess situations and devise perfect strategies. It conveys an almost supernatural ability to anticipate events and plan accordingly. Often used to praise a strategist or leader whose foresight proves remarkably accurate.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom is closely related to the classical expression '神机妙算,' which traces back to historical and literary traditions extolling military strategists of exceptional foresight. Figures such as Zhuge Liang of the Three Kingdoms period are commonly associated with such praise, as his campaigns exemplified uncanny predictive ability. Over time, the reversed form '妙算神机' emerged in vernacular literature and opera to convey the same admiration. While no single definitive source text is cited, it draws on the same cultural reverence for strategic wisdom found in works like 'Romance of the Three Kingdoms.' The phrase thus carries the weight of centuries of storytelling about brilliant tacticians.

💡 Example Usage

将军妙算神机，早料到敌军会从北面偷袭，已设下伏兵。

The general's marvelous foresight had already anticipated the enemy's northern ambush, and he had laid an ambush of his own.
