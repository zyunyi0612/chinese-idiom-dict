人极计生

rén jí jì shēng
Literal: "person, extreme, plan, born"
Home › Wisdom › 人极计生

📖 Meaning

When a person is pushed to the absolute limit or into a desperate predicament, resourcefulness and cunning naturally arise. It captures the idea that extreme pressure triggers ingenuity, forcing one to devise a way out.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom comes from the Ming dynasty vernacular story collection "Stories to Awaken the World" (醒世恒言) by Feng Menglong, in the tale "Fifteen Strings of Cash: A Joke Turns Tragic" (十五贯戏言成巧祸). A man is cornered and sees no escape until he spots a gleaming wood-chopping axe nearby. In that flash of desperation, he seizes the axe and strikes his opponent, an act the narrator describes as '人极计生' — a scheme born at the extreme. The phrase has since been used to describe sudden ingenuity under life-or-death pressure.

💡 Example Usage

他被人追到悬崖边，人极计生，抓起一根藤蔓荡到了对面。

Chased to the edge of a cliff, he grabbed a vine and swung to the other side — a plan born of desperation.
