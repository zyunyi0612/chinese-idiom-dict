意得志满

yì dé zhì mǎn
Literal: "desire, attained, will, full"
Home › Attitude › 意得志满

📖 Meaning

This idiom describes a state where one's desires and ambitions are fully realized, leaving one smugly satisfied and content. It conveys a sense of self-satisfaction that can border on complacency. The phrase is a variant of the more common form 志得意满, with the characters reordered.

📜 Origin Story

The idiom 意得志满 is a rearranged variant of the older and more frequently encountered phrase 志得意满, which has been used in Chinese literature for centuries to depict someone flushed with success. No single definitive source text is cited as its origin; rather, it arose through common literary usage as writers swapped the order of the two compounds 意得 (desires fulfilled) and 志满 (ambitions complete) for rhythmic or stylistic variation. Both forms carry the same meaning and have appeared in vernacular fiction and essays since at least the Ming and Qing dynasties.

💡 Example Usage

他刚升了职，正是意得志满的时候。

He just got promoted, and right now he is basking in smug self-satisfaction.
