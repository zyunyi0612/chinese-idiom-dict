同声相求

tóng shēng xiāng qiú
Literal: "same, sound, mutually, seek"
Home › Philosophy › 同声相求

📖 Meaning

People with similar interests, values, or views naturally gravitate toward and attract one another. It describes how like-minded individuals come together through shared affinities and common purpose.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom derives from the Book of Changes (周易), a foundational Chinese divination text from the Zhou Dynasty. The original phrase '同声相应，同气相求' appears in the commentary on the Qian hexagram, stating that similar sounds resonate together and similar energies seek one another. Over time, '同声相求' emerged as a condensed variant expressing the same philosophical idea. It reflects the ancient Chinese understanding of natural affinity and mutual attraction among kindred spirits.

💡 Example Usage

他们因对文学有共同热爱而同声相求，很快成了挚友。

Drawn together by their shared love of literature, they quickly became close friends.
