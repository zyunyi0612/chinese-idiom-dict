闭门埽轨

bì mén sào guǐ
Literal: "close, door, sweep, track"
Home › Attitude › 闭门埽轨

📖 Meaning

To shut one's door and sweep away the cart tracks in front of it. It means to cut off all social contact and refuse to receive guests. The idiom describes a deliberate choice of seclusion and withdrawal from social life.

📜 Origin Story

The idiom employs the archaic character 埽, a variant of 扫 (to sweep). The vivid image is of closing one's gate and sweeping away the ruts left by visitors' carriages, signaling that no guests are welcome. This concept of intentional seclusion pervades classical Chinese literature, particularly in accounts of recluses and scholar-officials who withdrew from public life to preserve their integrity. No single definitive source text is widely cited for this exact formulation. It reflects the traditional Chinese ideal of the hermit-scholar who chooses solitude over worldly entanglement.

💡 Example Usage

他退休后便闭门埽轨，不再与昔日同僚来往。

After retiring, he shut his door and severed all ties, no longer associating with his former colleagues.
