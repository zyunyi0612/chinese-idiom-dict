茫然若失

máng rán ruò shī
Literal: "blankly, as if, lost"
Home › Emotion › 茫然若失

📖 Meaning

Describes a dazed, distracted state where one feels as if something important has slipped away. It conveys mental absence and emotional emptiness, with the person appearing lost in thought and out of sorts. The expression captures a moment when shock or disappointment leaves the mind temporarily vacant.

📜 Origin Story

The idiom traces back to the classical text Mencius (孟子), specifically the chapter Teng Wen Gong I (滕文公上) from the pre-Qin period. In the original passage, when Xu Zi reported something to Yi Zi, Yi Zi fell silent for a moment before sighing that it was simply his fate. The collected commentary (集注) explains that the term '抚然' refers to the blank, self-loss appearance (茫然自失之貌) of someone unsettled by what they have heard. Over time, this image of a person stunned into vacant distraction was distilled into the four-character idiom used today.

💡 Example Usage

得知好友移民的消息后，他茫然若失地望着窗外。

After hearing the news that his close friend had emigrated, he stared blankly out the window as if he had lost something dear.
