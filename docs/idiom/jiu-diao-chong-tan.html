旧调重弹

jiù diào chóng tán
Literal: "old, tune, again, play"
Home › Communication › 旧调重弹

📖 Meaning

To rehash the same old ideas or arguments without any innovation. It describes someone who keeps bringing up the same worn-out topic or repeating the same old approach, like playing a stale melody over and over again.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom originates from the modern Chinese essayist Zhu Ziqing's essay '回来杂记' (Return Miscellany). In the essay, he observed that Beiping (present-day Beijing) had long been called a 'university city' and a 'cultural city,' and remarked that people were still '旧调重弹' — rehashing the same old tune — though this time it seemed to be played even louder. The phrase captures the weariness of hearing the same hollow labels repeated endlessly.

💡 Example Usage

他的发言毫无新意，不过是旧调重弹罢了。

His speech had nothing new to offer; it was merely rehashing the same old tune.
