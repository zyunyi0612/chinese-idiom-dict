熟能生巧

shú néng shēng qiǎo
Literal: "skilled, able, produce, skillful"
Home › Skill › 熟能生巧

📖 Meaning

Through repeated practice, one naturally develops skill and proficiency. The idiom teaches that mastery comes not from talent alone but from persistent effort and familiarity with a task.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom traces back to a story from the Song Dynasty recorded in Shen Kuo's Mengxi Bitan (Dream Pool Essays). A skilled archer named Chen Yaodi could hit a willow leaf at a hundred paces, yet an old oil peddler casually poured oil through the tiny hole of a copper coin without a single drop touching its edges. When asked, the peddler simply explained that it was nothing but repeated practice — illustrating that mastery arises from familiarity and relentless repetition rather than innate gift alone.

💡 Example Usage

只要每天坚持练习书法，熟能生巧，总有一天你能写出一手好字。

As long as you practice calligraphy every day, practice makes perfect — one day you will be able to write beautifully.
