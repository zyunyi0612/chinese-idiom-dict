急中生智

jí zhōng shēng zhì
Literal: "urgent, in, arise, wisdom"
Home › Wisdom › 急中生智

📖 Meaning

To suddenly come up with a clever idea or solution in a moment of urgency or crisis. It describes the ability to think quickly and resourcefully when under pressure. The phrase highlights how difficult circumstances can stimulate sharp, spontaneous thinking.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom originates from the preface to the Tang dynasty poet Bai Juyi's 'Twenty-Three Poems Matching Wei Zhi.' In the preface, Bai Juyi reflects on a friendly poetry contest with his fellow poet Yuan Zhen (courtesy name Wei Zhi), writing that when challenged by a worthy opponent, one's spirit rises and wisdom emerges in moments of urgency. The passage captures the idea that pressure and competition can sharpen the mind and spark ingenuity.

💡 Example Usage

大火封住了楼道，他急中生智，用湿棉被裹住身体冲了出去。

The fire had blocked the stairwell, but thinking quickly, he wrapped himself in a wet quilt and dashed to safety.
