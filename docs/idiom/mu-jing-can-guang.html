暮景残光

mù jǐng cán guāng
Literal: "dusk, scenery, fading, light"
Home › Condition › 暮景残光

📖 Meaning

Like the fading light of a setting sun. It is a metaphor for being old and frail, approaching the end of life. The idiom evokes the image of dim twilight to describe someone in their declining years.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom has no single definitive literary source and emerged as a folk-literary expression. It draws on the universal imagery of dusk and waning sunlight to symbolize the twilight of human life. Similar metaphors appear across classical Chinese poetry and prose, where evening and fading light conventionally represent old age and mortality.

💡 Example Usage

祖父已是暮景残光之身，却仍坚持每日读书写作。

Though my grandfather is in the twilight of his life, he still insists on reading and writing every day.
