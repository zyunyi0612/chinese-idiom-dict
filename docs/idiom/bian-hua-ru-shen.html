变化如神

biàn huà rú shén
Literal: "change, transform, like, divine"
Home › Method › 变化如神

📖 Meaning

This idiom describes changes that are so rapid and wondrous they seem supernatural. It is often used to praise someone's tactical flexibility or skillful adaptability in response to circumstances. The phrase conveys a sense of awe at how effortlessly and unpredictably a situation can be transformed.

📜 Origin Story

The phrase originates from a commentary by Pei Songzhi on the Records of the Three Kingdoms (Sanguozhi), specifically in the Biography of Emperor Wu of Wei. The commentary quotes the Book of Wei (Weishu), which describes Cao Cao's military leadership in unifying the realm. It notes that while Cao Cao broadly followed the strategies of Sunzi and Wu Qi, he devised ingenious tactics tailored to each situation, making his maneuvers change as wondrously as a god. The idiom thus praises extraordinary strategic adaptability.

💡 Example Usage

这位将军用兵变化如神，令敌人防不胜防。

This general's tactics changed as wondrously as the divine, leaving the enemy unable to defend against them.
