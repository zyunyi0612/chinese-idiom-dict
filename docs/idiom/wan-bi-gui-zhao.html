完璧归赵

wán bì guī zhào
Literal: "intact, jade, return, Zhao"
Home › Action › 完璧归赵

📖 Meaning

To return something to its rightful owner in perfect condition. It describes restoring an item without any damage or loss, often implying success in protecting something entrusted to you.

📜 Origin Story

During the Warring States period, the State of Zhao possessed the priceless Heshibi jade. The King of Qin demanded it in exchange for fifteen cities. Lin Xiangru, a Zhao envoy, took the jade to Qin but realized the king had no intention of honoring the deal. He cleverly threatened to smash the jade and kill himself, then secretly arranged for a subordinate to carry it back to Zhao intact, earning great honor for his state.

💡 Example Usage

借你的书我已经看完了，现在完璧归赵，谢谢。

I've finished reading the book I borrowed from you, so now I'm returning it in perfect condition. Thank you.
