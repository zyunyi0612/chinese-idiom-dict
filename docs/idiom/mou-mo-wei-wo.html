谋谟帷幄

móu mó wéi wò
Literal: "plan, counsel, curtained tent"
Home › Leadership › 谋谟帷幄

📖 Meaning

It means to devise battle strategies from within the command tent, i.e., to formulate military plans and then direct operations from afar. Figuratively extended to mean orchestrating overall plans, coordinating affairs, and exercising command or leadership.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom has no independently documented origin in classical texts; it is a variant formed by analogy with the better-known idiom 运筹帷幄 (yùn chóu wéi wò). That source expression comes from Sima Qian's Records of the Grand Historian (史记), where Emperor Gaozu of the Western Han dynasty praised his strategist Zhang Liang: 'devising strategies within the tent, and winning victories a thousand li away' (运筹策帷帐之中，决胜于千里之外). Over time, 谋谟 (to devise counsel and strategy) was substituted for 运筹 to create this parallel expression, which carries the same meaning of planning strategy and directing operations.

💡 Example Usage

他谋谟帷幄，统筹全局，终于带领团队走出困境。

He devised strategies from within the command tent, coordinated the whole situation, and finally led the team out of its difficulties.
