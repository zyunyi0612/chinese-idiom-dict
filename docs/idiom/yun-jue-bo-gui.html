云谲波诡

yún jué bō guǐ
Literal: "cloud, strange, wave, bizarre"
Home › Variety › 云谲波诡

📖 Meaning

Describing something as changeable and unpredictable like the ever-shifting shapes of clouds and waves. Originally used to describe the varied and intricate structure of grand buildings, it now mostly refers to events or situations that are highly variable and hard to anticipate.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom originates from the Han dynasty scholar Yang Xiong's rhapsody 'Sweet Springs Rhapsody' (甘泉赋), written in the 1st century BCE. In the text, Yang Xiong used it to describe the magnificent and intricately varied architecture of the imperial palace at Sweet Springs, whose structures rose and curved like clouds and waves in countless forms. Over time, the phrase shifted from describing architectural beauty to characterizing unpredictable and ever-changing circumstances in general.

💡 Example Usage

国际局势云谲波诡，谁也无法准确预测明天的走向。

The international situation is as unpredictable as shifting clouds and waves, and no one can accurately foretell where it will head tomorrow.
