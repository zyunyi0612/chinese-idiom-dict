雀屏中选

què píng zhòng xuǎn
Literal: "peacock, screen, hit, selected"
Home › Status › 雀屏中选

📖 Meaning

The peacock screen was hit and one was chosen. It refers to being selected as a son-in-law through a competitive process. The phrase originates from a story where a father tested suitors by having them shoot arrows at peacocks painted on a door screen.

📜 Origin Story

During the Sui Dynasty, Dou Yi, father of a talented daughter, painted two peacocks on a door screen and promised her hand to any suitor who could shoot both peacocks in the eyes with two arrows. Dozens of young nobles tried and failed. Li Yuan, who later became Emperor Gaozu of the Tang Dynasty, arrived and hit each peacock in one eye with his two arrows. Delighted, Dou Yi gave his daughter to Li Yuan in marriage, and she became Empress Dou of the Tang Dynasty.

💡 Example Usage

前来求婚的人很多，最终小李凭借出众的才华雀屏中选，成为王家的女婿。

Many suitors came to propose, but in the end Xiao Li was chosen as the son-in-law thanks to his outstanding talent.
