正本澄源

zhèng běn chéng yuán
Literal: "rectify, root, clarify, source"
Home › Method › 正本澄源

📖 Meaning

To rectify the fundamentals and clarify the source. It means to address problems at their root cause rather than merely treating symptoms, ensuring a thorough and lasting solution from the origin.

📜 Origin Story

From the Old Book of Tang, Annals of Emperor Gaozu (《旧唐书·高祖纪》). Emperor Gaozu (Li Yuan), founder of the Tang Dynasty, issued an edict on reforming Buddhism, arguing that to distinguish genuine from false and to preserve the true path, one must thoroughly examine and purify from the source. The phrase '正本澄源' was used to emphasize that lasting reform requires fixing problems at their foundation rather than superficially patching them. The Ming dynasty scholar Liu Bei (刘基) later cited it in his commentary on the Spring and Autumn Annals.

💡 Example Usage

治理环境污染必须正本澄源，从源头抓起才能见效。

To tackle environmental pollution, one must rectify the fundamentals and clarify the source; only by addressing it from the origin can real results be achieved.
