善与人交

shàn yǔ rén jiāo
Literal: "good at, with, people, associate"
Home › Communication › 善与人交

📖 Meaning

Being skilled at making friends and getting along with others. It describes someone who has a natural talent for building and maintaining interpersonal relationships. Confucius noted that such friendships endure and deepen with mutual respect over time.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom originates from the Analects, in the chapter 'Gongye Chang,' where Confucius praised Yan Pingzhong (晏平仲), a prime minister of the State of Qi during the Spring and Autumn Period. Confucius said, 'Yan Pingzhong is good at befriending others; the longer people know him, the more they respect him.' This highlights the enduring quality of genuine friendship built on mutual respect and sincerity.

💡 Example Usage

他性情温和，善与人交，无论走到哪里都能很快结交到朋友。

Mild-tempered and good at making friends, he can quickly befriend people wherever he goes.
