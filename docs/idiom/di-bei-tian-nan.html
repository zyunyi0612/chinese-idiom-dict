地北天南

dì běi tiān nán
Literal: "earth, north, sky, south"
Home › Distance › 地北天南

📖 Meaning

This idiom means everywhere, all over the place, or far and wide. It describes covering vast distances in all directions, being scattered across the world, or wandering without a fixed home. It conveys a sense of boundlessness and the vastness of the world.

📜 Origin Story

The idiom comes from the Qing Dynasty opera "The Peach Blossom Fan" (桃花扇) by Kong Shangren (孔尚任), specifically from the scene "Inscribing a Painting" (题画). The original line reads: "地北天南蓬转，巫云楚雨丝牵," which describes lovers separated by vast distances, wandering like tumbleweed blown from north to south, yet still connected by threads of longing like clouds over Mount Wu and rain in the Chu region. The phrase captures both physical separation and emotional connection across immense space.

💡 Example Usage

他为了寻找失散多年的亲人，走遍了地北天南。

He traveled far and wide, searching everywhere for his long-lost family.
