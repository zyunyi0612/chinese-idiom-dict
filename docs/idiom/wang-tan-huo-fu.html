妄谈祸福

wàng tán huò fú
Literal: "recklessly talk, disaster and fortune"
Home › Communication › 妄谈祸福

📖 Meaning

To speak of good and bad fortune without any basis or evidence. The idiom criticizes people who casually predict future outcomes or divine fortunes based on nothing but speculation or hearsay. It carries a strong negative tone, implying that such talk is groundless, irresponsible, and unworthy of belief.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom has no documented origin and does not trace back to any specific classical text, dynasty, or historical figure. It is formed by combining 妄 (reckless, groundless), 谈 (to talk), and 祸福 (misfortune and fortune), a phrase frequently seen in classical literature such as the Book of Changes. As a compound expression, it draws on the long-standing Chinese tradition of fortune-telling and divination, where predicting one's fate was a serious matter. Over time it came to describe those who speak of luck and calamity casually, without any real basis.

💡 Example Usage

算命先生不过是妄谈祸福，千万别当真。

The fortune-teller is only making groundless predictions about our fortunes, so don't take him seriously at all.
