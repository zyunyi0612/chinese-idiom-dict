如原以偿

rú yuán yǐ cháng
Literal: "as, original, thereby, fulfill"
Home › Success › 如原以偿

📖 Meaning

This idiom means that one's wishes or desires are fulfilled and realized. It describes the satisfaction of achieving what one originally hoped for. The phrase conveys a sense of contentment when long-held aspirations finally come to fruition.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom does not trace to a specific classical text and has no recorded origin story. It is a variant of the more common idiom 如愿以偿 (rú yuàn yǐ cháng), which appears in Qing dynasty literature such as Wu Jianren's 'Twenty Years of Strange Things Witnessed.' The character 原 (yuán, meaning 'original') replaces the standard 愿 (yuàn, meaning 'wish'), lending the phrase a nuance of fulfilling one's original intention rather than merely one's wishes. Both forms share the same core meaning of desires being realized.

💡 Example Usage

经过多年努力，他终于如原以偿，考上了理想的大学。

After years of hard work, he finally got his wish fulfilled and was admitted to his ideal university.
