令人莫测

lìng rén mò cè
Literal: "make, people, cannot, fathom"
Home › Clarity › 令人莫测

📖 Meaning

Describes a situation or person so complex, mysterious, or changeable that others cannot figure out or predict it. It conveys a sense of inscrutability that leaves observers unable to guess what comes next.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom has no single classical origin story; it is a descriptive four-character phrase assembled from common literary components. '令' means 'to cause,' '人' means 'people,' '莫' means 'cannot,' and '测' means 'to fathom or measure.' Such verb-object compounds became widespread in vernacular fiction and late imperial commentary, where narrators needed a concise way to mark a character's or event's unpredictability. It functions more as a generalized descriptive phrase than as an allusion.

💡 Example Usage

他的真实意图令人莫测，谁也不知道他下一步会做什么。

His true intentions are unfathomable; no one knows what he will do next.
