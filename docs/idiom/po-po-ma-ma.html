婆婆妈妈

pó pó mā mā
Literal: "grandma, grandma, mama, mama"
Home › Attitude › 婆婆妈妈

📖 Meaning

Describes someone who is fussy over trivial details, nagging, and wordy in speech and action. It can also describe someone who is overly sentimental or emotionally fragile.

📜 Origin Story

The phrase draws on the image of elder women (婆婆妈妈) chattering and fussing over petty household matters. It appears in modern Chinese literature, notably cited by Yu Qiuyu in his essay collection 'Cultural Journey' (文化苦旅), in the chapter on Shanghai people, where he refers to lower-class Shanghai residents who liked to gossip in a '婆婆妈妈' manner. There is no single classical-dynasty origin story; the expression evolved from colloquial usage into a common idiom for indecisive, over-fussy behavior.

💡 Example Usage

他办起事来婆婆妈妈的，一点魄力都没有。

He handles things in a fussy, wishy-washy way and has no decisiveness at all.
