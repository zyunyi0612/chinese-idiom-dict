白山黑水

bái shān hēi shuǐ
Literal: "white mountain, black water"
Home › Distance › 白山黑水

📖 Meaning

Refers to Changbai Mountain (长白山) and the Heilongjiang River (黑龙江), collectively symbolizing Northeast China. It is commonly used to describe the northeastern region of China, its landscape, and its cultural heritage.

📜 Origin Story

The phrase originates from the 'History of Jin' (《金史·世纪》), which records the birthplace of the Jurchen people as having the Huntong River and Changbai Mountain. The Huntong River was also called the Heilongjiang (Black Dragon River), giving rise to the expression 'white mountain and black water.' The term has since become a poetic shorthand for the Manchurian homeland and the broader Northeast China region.

💡 Example Usage

白山黑水之间孕育了无数英勇的儿女，他们为保卫家园付出了巨大牺牲。

Between the white mountains and black waters, countless brave sons and daughters were nurtured, who made great sacrifices to defend their homeland.
