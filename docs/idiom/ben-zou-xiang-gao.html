奔走相告

bēn zǒu xiāng gào
Literal: "run, walk, mutually, tell"
Home › Communication › 奔走相告

📖 Meaning

This idiom describes people running about to share important news with one another. It conveys a sense of urgency and excitement when significant information spreads quickly through a community.

📜 Origin Story

The phrase originates from the 'Guoyu' (Discourses of the States), a text from the Spring and Autumn period compiled in the Warring States era. In the section 'Discourses of Lu,' it records the phrase '士有陪乘，告奔走也,' referring to the practice of attendants accompanying a ruler and reporting urgent matters by running to relay the news. Over time, the expression evolved to describe the common scene of people hurrying to spread major news among themselves.

💡 Example Usage

胜利的消息传来，人们奔走相告，街头巷尾一片欢腾。

When the news of victory arrived, people rushed about telling one another, and the streets were filled with jubilation.
