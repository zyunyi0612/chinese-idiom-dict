尤云殢雨

yóu yún zhì yǔ
Literal: "linger, cloud, entangle, rain"
Home › Lifestyle › 尤云殢雨

📖 Meaning

A classical euphemism describing a couple deeply absorbed in sensual intimacy and romantic passion. The phrase draws on the traditional imagery of 'clouds and rain' (云雨), an age-old Chinese metaphor for sexual union. It conveys being luxuriously entangled in physical love between a man and a woman.

📜 Origin Story

The idiom builds upon the enduring Chinese poetic metaphor of 'clouds and rain' (云雨) as a symbol of sexual intimacy. That metaphor traces back to the ancient legend of the Goddess of Mount Wu (巫山神女), first recorded in 'Gaotang Fu' (高唐赋) by the Warring States poet Song Yu (宋玉), where the goddess describes herself as 'morning clouds and evening rain' (朝云暮雨). The specific four-character form '尤云殢雨'—with 尤 and 殢 both suggesting lingering, indulgent attachment—became a favored expression in Song dynasty lyric poetry and Yuan dynasty drama (元曲), where writers depicted romantic and erotic love between lovers.

💡 Example Usage

他们成婚之后，整日尤云殢雨，沉浸于甜蜜之中。

After they married, the two spent their days lost in passionate intimacy, utterly immersed in sweet bliss.
