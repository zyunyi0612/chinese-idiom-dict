黄发台背

huáng fā tái bèi
Literal: "yellow hair, mackerel back"
Home › Status › 黄发台背

📖 Meaning

Refers to elderly people of advanced age. 'Yellow hair' describes the phenomenon where a very old person's white hair turns yellowish, while 'mackerel back' (台通鲐) refers to mottled spots on the back resembling a mackerel fish's markings. The term is used to denote venerable old age and longevity.

📜 Origin Story

The expression derives from classical Chinese physical descriptions of the elderly. In traditional observation, extremely aged individuals sometimes develop yellowish hair and mottled skin on their backs, likened to the spotted markings of a mackerel fish (鲐鱼). The character 台 functions as a loan character for 鲐 (mackerel). Such vivid bodily imagery was commonly used in ancient Chinese texts to honor and identify the elderly, reflecting the culture's reverence for longevity.

💡 Example Usage

村里黄发台背的老人们都来参加寿宴。

The elderly villagers, all advanced in age, came to attend the longevity banquet.
