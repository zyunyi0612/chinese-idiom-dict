鲜眉亮眼

xiān méi liàng yǎn
Literal: "fresh, eyebrow, bright, eye"
Home › Quality › 鲜眉亮眼

📖 Meaning

Literally 'fresh eyebrows and bright eyes,' describing someone with clear, well-defined brows and bright eyes. It is used to praise a person's attractive and refined facial appearance, similar to saying someone has delicate and handsome features.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom is a descriptive folk expression with no single identifiable literary source. It belongs to the tradition of pairing facial features—eyebrows and eyes—to convey beauty, a convention seen in earlier classical phrases such as 眉清目秀. The word 鲜 (fresh, vivid) emphasizes the lively, clear quality of the brows, while 亮 (bright) highlights the sparkle of the eyes. Over time it has been used colloquially in vernacular literature and everyday speech to compliment good looks.

💡 Example Usage

那小伙子鲜眉亮眼，举止斯文，很讨长辈喜欢。

That young man has fresh brows and bright eyes and carries himself with such gentle manners that the elders all like him.
