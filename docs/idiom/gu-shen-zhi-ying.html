孤身只影

gū shēn zhī yǐng
Literal: "solitary, body, single, shadow"
Home › Condition › 孤身只影

📖 Meaning

Describes a person who is utterly alone, with no family or companions — so isolated that only their own shadow accompanies them. It conveys a deep sense of loneliness and abandonment, often used for orphans or those who have lost all kin.

📜 Origin Story

The idiom originates from the Yuan dynasty playwright Guan Hanqing's famous tragedy 'The Injustice to Dou E' (《窦娥冤》), Act 3. The heroine Dou E, falsely accused and condemned to death, laments her fate in a heart-wrenching aria, crying out that she is '孤身只影' — utterly alone with no relatives to turn to. Guan Hanqing uses this phrase to underscore the depth of her isolation and the cruelty of her unjust punishment. The image of a solitary figure with only their shadow for company became a powerful literary motif for orphanhood and abandonment in classical Chinese literature.

💡 Example Usage

他孤身只影漂泊海外，举目无亲。

He drifted overseas all alone, with not a single relative in sight.
