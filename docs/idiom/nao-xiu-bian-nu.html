恼羞变怒

nǎo xiū biàn nù
Literal: "annoyed, ashamed, turn, angry"
Home › Emotion › 恼羞变怒

📖 Meaning

To become enraged out of extreme shame and embarrassment when one cannot save face. It is a variant of the more common idiom "恼羞成怒". The phrase describes someone who, unable to bear humiliation, lashes out in anger rather than accept the embarrassment.

📜 Origin Story

No specific literary origin is recorded for this idiom. It is a variant form of "恼羞成怒," which arose in vernacular fiction and oral storytelling traditions to depict the psychological progression from shame to fury. The expression captures a universal human reaction where deep embarrassment curdles into hostility. It appears across modern literary works and everyday speech as a synonymous alternative to the standard form.

💡 Example Usage

被当众揭穿谎言后，他恼羞变怒，摔门而去。

After his lie was exposed in public, he flew into a rage out of shame and stormed out, slamming the door.
