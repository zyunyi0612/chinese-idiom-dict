温香艳玉

wēn xiāng yàn yù
Literal: "warm, fragrant, beautiful, jade"
Home › Quality › 温香艳玉

📖 Meaning

A metaphor describing the beautiful qualities and charm of a woman. The phrase evokes warmth, fragrance, beauty, and the preciousness of jade to portray feminine allure. It is used in classical literature to praise a woman's graceful appearance and gentle disposition.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom draws from classical Chinese poetic traditions that compared beautiful women to fragrant blossoms and precious jade. The imagery of warmth and fragrance recalls descriptions found in Tang and Song dynasty poetry, where scholars often likened court ladies to flowers and jade. No single definitive literary source is recorded for this exact four-character combination. It reflects the broader aesthetic vocabulary of classical Chinese literature, which frequently paired sensory descriptors of scent and texture to celebrate feminine beauty.

💡 Example Usage

她容貌清丽，举止优雅，堪称温香艳玉，令人倾慕不已。

With her lovely features and graceful bearing, she could be called a vision of warmth, fragrance, and jade-like beauty, captivating all who saw her.
