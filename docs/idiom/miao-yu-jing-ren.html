妙语惊人

miào yǔ jīng rén
Literal: "wonderful, words, startle, people"
Home › Communication › 妙语惊人

📖 Meaning

This idiom describes remarkably clever, witty, or profound speech that astonishes its audience. It praises words so well-crafted or insightful that listeners are struck with surprise and admiration. It is commonly used to commend someone's eloquence at gatherings, in conversation, or in writing.

📜 Origin Story

Unlike many classical Chinese idioms, 妙语惊人 does not trace to a single canonical text or historical anecdote. It is a descriptive compound formed from common literary vocabulary—'妙语' (clever words) paired with '惊人' (to astonish people)—that crystallizes a value long cherished in Chinese rhetorical and literary tradition: the power of eloquent speech to move and surprise. The admiration of witty, startling language runs through Chinese poetry criticism and essay commentary from the Tang and Song dynasties onward. It remains a familiar expression of praise for spontaneous eloquence and sharp wit.

💡 Example Usage

他在会上的一番妙语惊人，让全场为之折服。

His astonishingly witty remarks at the meeting won over the entire audience.
