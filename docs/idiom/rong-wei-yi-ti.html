融为一体

róng wéi yī tǐ
Literal: "melt, become, one, body"
Home › Condition › 融为一体

📖 Meaning

To blend or merge into a single whole. It describes how different things combine so seamlessly and naturally that they form one unified entity, as if they were always one. Often used for relationships, styles, or elements that harmonize perfectly.

📜 Origin Story

The phrase traces back to the pre-Qin period, specifically to Guan Zhong's work "Guanzi·Qifa" (《管子·七法》), which discusses governance as an integrated whole. The text states that when governance operates as one unified system, orders can be issued and laws clarified effectively. Over time, the expression evolved beyond political theory to describe any seamless fusion of distinct elements into a cohesive unity.

💡 Example Usage

音乐与画面融为一体，营造出震撼人心的氛围。

The music and visuals blended into one, creating a breathtaking atmosphere.
