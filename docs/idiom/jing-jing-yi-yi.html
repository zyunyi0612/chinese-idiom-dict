兢兢翼翼

jīng jīng yì yì
Literal: "cautious, cautious, reverent, reverent"
Home › Attitude › 兢兢翼翼

📖 Meaning

Describes being extremely cautious and diligent in one's work and conduct. It conveys a sense of careful attentiveness and conscientious effort in handling affairs, similar to the more common idiom 兢兢业业.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom originates from a eulogy written by Cao Zhi (192–232 CE), a prince of the Cao Wei state during the Three Kingdoms period. In his 'Eulogy for Empress Dowager Bian' (卞太后诔), Cao Zhi praised the empress dowager for her tireless diligence in managing state affairs, describing her as working '兢兢翼翼' with constant caution and reverent care. The phrase combines 兢兢 (fearful and cautious) with 翼翼 (reverent and attentive), emphasizing both vigilance and earnest dedication.

💡 Example Usage

他在岗位上兢兢翼翼，从不敢有丝毫懈怠。

He worked at his post with utmost caution and diligence, never daring to slack off in the slightest.
