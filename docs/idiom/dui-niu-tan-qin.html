对牛弹琴

duì niú tán qín
Literal: "play lute to cow"
Home › Communication › 对牛弹琴

📖 Meaning

To waste one's words or efforts on someone who cannot understand or appreciate them. Talking over someone's head.

📜 Origin Story

A musician played beautiful music for a cow, but the cow only cared about eating grass—the music was wasted on a listener who couldn't understand it.

💡 Example Usage

跟他讲哲学就是白费口舌，对牛弹琴。

Explaining philosophy to him is like playing the lute to a cow—a waste of breath.
