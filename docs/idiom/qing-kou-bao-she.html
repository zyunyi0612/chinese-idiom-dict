轻口薄舌

qīng kǒu báo shé
Literal: "light, mouth, thin, tongue"
Home › Communication › 轻口薄舌

📖 Meaning

To speak lightly and carelessly without restraint or thought. It describes someone who is glib, loose-tongued, or prone to making flip remarks. Often used to criticize a person who speaks without considering the consequences of their words.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom appears in various vernacular Chinese literary works as a traditional descriptive phrase rather than tracing to a single definitive source. The component characters '轻' (light, frivolous) and '薄' (thin, lacking depth) both carry the sense of treating something with insufficient seriousness or weight. Similar expressions such as '轻嘴薄舌' appear in Ming and Qing dynasty vernacular novels, where they were commonly used to describe servants, gossips, or characters prone to loose talk.

💡 Example Usage

他这人轻口薄舌，什么秘密都藏不住。

He is so loose-tongued that no secret can stay hidden with him.
