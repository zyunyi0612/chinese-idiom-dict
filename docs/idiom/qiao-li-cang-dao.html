鞘里藏刀

qiào lǐ cáng dāo
Literal: "scabbard, inside, hide, knife"
Home › Attitude › 鞘里藏刀

📖 Meaning

To hide a sharp blade inside a scabbard. It is a metaphor for concealing murderous intent or hostile designs beneath a calm exterior. The phrase describes someone who appears harmless but secretly harbors lethal intentions.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom does not derive from a single classical text but is formed from the literal image of a blade concealed within its sheath. It draws on the long-standing Chinese metaphorical tradition of associating hidden weapons with concealed malice. Unlike related idioms that trace to specific historical anecdotes, this phrase is a descriptive compound built from common characters. Its usage reflects the cultural wariness toward those who mask aggression behind a placid facade.

💡 Example Usage

他表面和善，实则鞘里藏刀，令人难以防备。

He seems friendly on the surface, but in reality he hides a knife in his scabbard, making him impossible to guard against.
