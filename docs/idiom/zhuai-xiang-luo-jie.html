拽巷攞街

zhuài xiàng luǒ jiē
Literal: "drag, alley, disperse, street"
Home › Communication › 拽巷攞街

📖 Meaning

To shout loudly and cause a commotion that disturbs the neighbors. It describes someone making a noisy scene in public, drawing attention from the entire neighborhood. Often used to criticize loud, quarrelsome, or theatrical behavior.

📜 Origin Story

The idiom first appears in the Yuan dynasty zaju play 'The Gold Phoenix Hairpin' (《金凤钗》) by playwright Zheng Tingyu (郑廷玉). In its third act, the line reads '全不想离乡背井，动不动拽巷攞街' — criticizing someone who never reflects on having left home and instead constantly stirs up noisy commotions in the streets and alleys. The phrase graphically conveys a person dragging their quarrel through every lane and street, disturbing the whole neighborhood.

💡 Example Usage

他因一点小事就在院里拽巷攞街，惹得四邻不安。

Over a trivial matter, he made such a noisy scene in the courtyard that all the neighbors were disturbed.
