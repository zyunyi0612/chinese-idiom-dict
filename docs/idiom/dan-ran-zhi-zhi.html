淡然置之

dàn rán zhì zhī
Literal: "indifferently, place, it"
Home › Attitude › 淡然置之

📖 Meaning

To set something aside with complete indifference or cold detachment. It describes an attitude of not caring at all about something, treating it as if it hardly matters.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom does not trace back to a single specific literary source or historical anecdote. It is a compound expression formed from '淡然' (indifferently, aloofly) and '置之' (set it aside), both of which appear separately in various classical texts. The phrase reflects a traditional Chinese philosophical ideal of detachment and composure, echoing Daoist and Confucius-influenced themes of remaining unmoved by worldly affairs. Over time it became a common expression for describing someone who disregards or ignores something without emotional engagement.

💡 Example Usage

面对同事的无理指责，他只是淡然置之，继续做自己的工作。

Faced with his colleague's unreasonable accusations, he simply brushed them aside indifferently and continued with his own work.
