喜气洋洋

xǐ qì yáng yáng
Literal: "joy, air, overflowing, overflowing"
Home › Emotion › 喜气洋洋

📖 Meaning

Full of joy and happiness, with a cheerful expression or atmosphere that radiates outward. It describes a person beaming with delight or an occasion filled with festive, joyous spirit.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom originates from Fan Zhongyan's famous essay 'Record of Yueyang Tower' (岳阳楼记) during the Northern Song Dynasty. In the passage, Fan describes ascending the tower on a fine day, feeling one's mind expand and spirit soar, all honors and disgraces forgotten, as one raises a cup to the wind — utterly filled with joy. The phrase '其喜洋洋者矣' (utterly overflowing with joy) was later condensed into the four-character idiom 喜气洋洋.

💡 Example Usage

春节到了，家家户户喜气洋洋地迎接新年。

With the Spring Festival arriving, every household welcomes the New Year overflowing with joy.
