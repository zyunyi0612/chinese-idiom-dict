随寓而安

suí yù ér ān
Literal: "follow, reside, and, peace"
Home › Attitude › 随寓而安

📖 Meaning

To adapt to one's environment and remain content in any situation or circumstance. It reflects a philosophical acceptance of life's changes and the ability to find inner peace regardless of external conditions.

📜 Origin Story

The exact origin of this idiom is not clearly documented in classical texts. It is a variant form of the more common idiom '随遇而安' (suí yù ér ān), which shares the same pronunciation and meaning. The phrase is believed to have appeared in Song Dynasty scholar Zhu Xi's annotations on the works of Mencius, where he used it to describe the sage's ability to remain undisturbed by poverty or wealth alike.

💡 Example Usage

无论身处何地，他都能随寓而安，从不抱怨。

No matter where he finds himself, he can always adapt and be content, never complaining.
