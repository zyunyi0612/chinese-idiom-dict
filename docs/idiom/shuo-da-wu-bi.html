硕大无比

shuò dà wú bǐ
Literal: "large, big, without, compare"
Home › Quality › 硕大无比

📖 Meaning

Describes something exceptionally large, so great in size that nothing can rival or compare with it. It is used to emphasize the sheer magnitude or enormity of an object, creature, or phenomenon.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom has no single classical origin story; it is a descriptive four-character compound built on the earlier idiom 硕大无朋, which appears in the Shijing (Book of Songs) of the Zhou dynasty. The character 硕 denotes largeness or greatness, while 无比 literally means 'without comparison.' Over time the phrase became a common intensifying expression used in both literary and vernacular Chinese to convey unmatched size.

💡 Example Usage

那头硕大无比的蓝鲸从海面跃起，激起千层浪花。

The incomparably enormous blue whale leapt from the sea surface, sending up towering plumes of spray.
