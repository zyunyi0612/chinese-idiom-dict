钓游之地

diào yóu zhī dì
Literal: "fishing, roaming, of, place"
Home › Lifestyle › 钓游之地

📖 Meaning

A place where one fished and played as a child, typically referring to one's hometown or the place where one grew up. It evokes a sense of nostalgia for the carefree days of childhood spent in familiar natural surroundings.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom originates from Han Yu, a prominent Tang Dynasty writer and philosopher, in his 'Preface to Sending Off Yang Shao Yin' (《送杨少尹序》). In the text, Han Yu describes a returning official pointing to trees and streams, saying, 'This tree was planted by my ancestors; this stream and this hill are where I fished and roamed as a child.' The phrase captures the deep emotional bond between a person and the landscape of their youth, which later became a literary trope for expressing nostalgia for one's hometown.

💡 Example Usage

时隔三十年重返钓游之地，一切已物是人非。

After thirty years, returning to the place where I fished and roamed as a child, I found everything changed beyond recognition.
