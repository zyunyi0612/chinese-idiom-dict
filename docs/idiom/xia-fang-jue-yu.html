遐方绝域

xiá fāng jué yù
Literal: "far, place, cut off, region"
Home › Distance › 遐方绝域

📖 Meaning

Refers to remote, out-of-the-way regions far from the centers of civilization. The idiom describes places that are distant, isolated, and difficult to reach, often evoking a sense of wildness or desolation.

📜 Origin Story

There is no single documented origin story or attributed author for this idiom. It is a classical literary compound built from four components: 遐 (distant), 方 (region), 绝 (severed/isolated), and 域 (territory). The expression likely emerged through the cumulative usage of classical writers describing frontier lands, and it has been preserved in later literary Chinese as a standard way to refer to remote borderlands.

💡 Example Usage

他辞别亲友，独自远赴遐方绝域，追寻心中的理想。

He bid farewell to his family and friends and traveled alone to a remote, isolated frontier in pursuit of the ideal in his heart.
