评功摆好

píng gōng bǎi hǎo
Literal: "evaluate, merit, display, good"
Home › Attitude › 评功摆好

📖 Meaning

To assess achievements and lay out one's good qualities or accomplishments. It describes a situation where people review and highlight their merits, often during performance evaluations or self-assessments, sometimes with the implication of self-promotion.

📜 Origin Story

This is a modern Chinese expression with no classical literary source. It emerged from everyday workplace and political language, particularly common in the context of performance reviews and self-criticism sessions in mid-20th century China. The phrase encapsulates the practice of formally evaluating one's contributions and presenting one's merits for recognition.

💡 Example Usage

年终总结时，大家都在评功摆好，却很少有人反思自己的不足。

During the year-end review, everyone was busy evaluating their merits and listing their good deeds, but few reflected on their shortcomings.
