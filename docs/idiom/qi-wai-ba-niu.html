七歪八扭

qī wāi bā niǔ
Literal: "seven, crooked, eight, twisted"
Home › Quality › 七歪八扭

📖 Meaning

Describes something that is crooked, slanted, and out of alignment. It conveys an irregular, uneven appearance, commonly applied to physical objects such as trees, handwriting, or structures that are not straight or orderly.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom originates from the Qing dynasty novel The Three Heroes and Five Gallants (三侠五义) by Shi Yukun (石玉昆), Chapter 84, which describes a scene of devastating flood damage where houses were shattered and trees left crooked and twisted. A later notable usage appears in Liu E's (刘鹗) The Travels of Lao Can (老残游记), Chapter 12, where the protagonist writes on a wall in an uneven,歪歪扭扭 manner. The idiom uses the numeral-contrast pattern (七…八…) common in Chinese to convey a sense of disorder and irregularity.

💡 Example Usage

那阵大风吹过，院子里的小树都被刮得七歪八扭。

After the fierce wind passed, the young trees in the courtyard were all blown crooked and twisted.
