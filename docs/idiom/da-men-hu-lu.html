打闷葫芦

dǎ mèn hú lu
Literal: "hit, stuffy, gourd"
Home › Knowledge › 打闷葫芦

📖 Meaning

To puzzle over something baffling or hard to decipher, like trying to guess what is inside a sealed gourd. It describes being kept in suspense or unable to make sense of cryptic words and events. Often used when someone is left guessing without getting a straight answer.

📜 Origin Story

The phrase comes from Qing dynasty author Cao Xueqin's classic novel Dream of the Red Chamber, Chapter 5. In the scene, the fairy Disenchantment fears Baoyu is about to learn heavenly secrets, so she closes the register of destinies and laughingly tells him to follow her to enjoy the wondrous sights instead of pondering an unsolvable riddle there. A sealed gourd that cannot be opened or seen through became a metaphor for matters that leave people guessing. The expression later spread as a vivid image of being kept in the dark.

💡 Example Usage

他话只说了一半，让我在这儿打闷葫芦。

He only said half of what he meant, leaving me here puzzling over the riddle.
