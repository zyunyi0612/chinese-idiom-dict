宿水飡风

xiǔ shuǐ cān fēng
Literal: "lodge overnight, water, eat, wind"
Home › Condition › 宿水飡风

📖 Meaning

Describes the extreme hardship of travel or life in the wild, where one is forced to sleep wherever one can and endure wind, rain, and exposure. It conveys the toil and privation of long journeys made under open skies. It is a variant form of the idiom 宿水餐风, meaning essentially the same thing.

📜 Origin Story

No definitive textual source has been identified for this exact written form, so no dynasty or figure can be named with certainty. The character 飡 is an archaic variant of 餐 (to eat), which makes 宿水飡风 essentially a calligraphic variant of 宿水餐风. This idiom belongs to a family of expressions such as 风餐露宿 and 餐风宿露 that appear in classical poetry and fiction, especially from the Tang through the Ming-Qing periods, to describe travelers enduring harsh outdoor conditions. Because the constituent characters are interchangeable, the idiom carries the same meaning as its more common counterparts, though its precise first appearance cannot be traced.

💡 Example Usage

古人远行，宿水飡风，备尝艰辛，方得一览山河之壮。

In ancient times, travelers endured the hardship of sleeping in the open and braving the wind before they could finally behold the grandeur of mountains and rivers.
