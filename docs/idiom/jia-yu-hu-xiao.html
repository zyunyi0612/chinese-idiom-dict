家喻户晓

jiā yù hù xiǎo
Literal: "family, inform, household, know"
Home › Communication › 家喻户晓

📖 Meaning

Known by every household; widely known and universally recognized. It describes something—often news, a name, or a concept—that has spread so thoroughly through a community or society that virtually everyone is aware of it.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom comes from the Song dynasty. It was popularized by the Song scholar and official Liu Kezhuang (刘克庄) in his literary writings. The phrase uses 喻 (yù, to inform/make known) and 晓 (xiǎo, to understand/know) to convey that information has been communicated to every family and understood by every household, emphasizing the thoroughness of its dissemination.

💡 Example Usage

孙悟空的故事在中国家喻户晓，几乎每个孩子都知道。

The story of Sun Wukong is known to every household in China; almost every child knows it.
