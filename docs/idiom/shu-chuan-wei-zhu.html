束椽为柱

shù chuán wéi zhù
Literal: "bind, rafters, as, pillar"
Home › Quality › 束椽为柱

📖 Meaning

Binding rafters together to serve as structural pillars. It is a metaphor for using small or inadequate materials for a major purpose — assigning someone or something unqualified to an important role.

📜 Origin Story

No specific literary source is recorded for this idiom. It draws on the imagery of traditional Chinese timber architecture, where thick pillars bear the building's main load while slender rafters merely support the roof tiles. Bundling thin rafters to replace solid pillars would be a makeshift, structurally unsound substitution. The phrase thus came to illustrate the dangerous mismatch between capability and responsibility.

💡 Example Usage

让一个毫无经验的实习生主持国家级工程，无异于束椽为柱。

Putting an inexperienced intern in charge of a national-level project is like binding rafters to serve as pillars.
