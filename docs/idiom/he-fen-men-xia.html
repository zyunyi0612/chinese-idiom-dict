河汾门下

hé fén mén xià
Literal: "Yellow River, Fen River, beneath the gate"
Home › Learning › 河汾门下

📖 Meaning

Describes a renowned teacher's school that produces an abundance of outstanding disciples. Named after the great Sui dynasty scholar Wang Tong, whose academy between the Yellow River and Fen Rivers nurtured many founders of the Tang dynasty. Used to praise a master under whose guidance talented students emerge in great numbers.

📜 Origin Story

In the late Sui dynasty, the great Confucian scholar Wang Tong (also known as Wenzhongzi) established an academy for teaching between the Yellow River and the Fen River. Over a thousand students came from far and wide to study under him. His disciples included Fang Xuanling, Du Ruhui, Wei Zheng, Li Jing, Xue Shou, and Wen Daya, who all became founding ministers and heroes of the early Tang dynasty. People of the time called these brilliant students 'beneath the gate of the Yellow River and Fen River,' and the phrase came to signify a master's school that turns out talent in abundance.

💡 Example Usage

这所名校名师荟萃，河汾门下，培养出无数栋梁之才。

This prestigious school gathers renowned teachers; like the school of the Yellow River and Fen, it has cultivated countless pillars of talent.
