高枕勿忧

gāo zhěn wù yōu
Literal: "high, pillow, do not, worry"
Home › Attitude › 高枕勿忧

📖 Meaning

An expression meaning to rest easy without worry, essentially identical to 高枕无忧 (gāo zhěn wú yōu). It describes a state of complete peace of mind where one can relax and sleep soundly without any concerns. The phrase conveys confidence that a situation is secure and no further vigilance is needed.

📜 Origin Story

This phrase appears in 《四游记》(The Four Journeys), a Ming dynasty novel combining four popular travel tales. In the chapter titled '玉帝起来赛宝通明会' (The Jade Emperor Holds a Treasure Competition at the Tongming Assembly), a Buddhist monk (比丘) comforts his mother, saying 'Mother, rest easy without worry,' and then quotes the old proverb 'When soldiers come, generals resist them; when water comes, earth blocks it.' The passage conveys that for every difficulty there is a corresponding remedy, so there is no need for undue anxiety.

💡 Example Usage

有我在此坐镇，母亲尽可高枕勿忧。

With me holding the fort here, Mother can rest completely at ease.
