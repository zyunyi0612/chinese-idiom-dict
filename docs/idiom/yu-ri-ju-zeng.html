与日俱增

yǔ rì jù zēng
Literal: "with, day, together, increase"
Home › Condition › 与日俱增

📖 Meaning

To grow steadily with each passing day. It describes something—such as a feeling, influence, or problem—that increases continuously over time, becoming stronger or more pronounced as days go by.

📜 Origin Story

The idiom traces back to classical Chinese prose rather than a single historical event. It appears in the works of Song dynasty literati, who used the phrase to describe ever-growing sentiments or phenomena. The structure parallels earlier Han dynasty expressions about things accumulating day by day. It has been a standard literary phrase in Chinese writing for over a thousand years, used by scholars and officials alike to convey the idea of relentless, daily growth.

💡 Example Usage

随着环境污染的加剧，人们对环保意识的重视程度与日俱增。

As environmental pollution worsens, people's awareness of environmental protection has been growing with each passing day.
