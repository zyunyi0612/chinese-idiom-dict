莫之与京

mò zhī yǔ jīng
Literal: "none, it, compare with, great"
Home › Quality › 莫之与京

📖 Meaning

This idiom means that something is so vast, high, or great that nothing can be compared with it. It describes being foremost, unrivaled, and without equal. It is also written in the variant form 大莫与京.

📜 Origin Story

No recorded origin story or source text has been preserved for this idiom, so its precise provenance is unknown. It follows the classical grammatical pattern seen in expressions like 无与伦比, where 之 serves as a proposed object and 与 means 'to compare.' Here 京 carries the archaic sense of 'great' or 'lofty,' the same root meaning found in words referring to a great capital city. The idiom functions as a literary, formal way of saying something is matchless in grandeur or stature.

💡 Example Usage

此城之繁华，莫之与京。

The prosperity of this city is so great that nothing else can compare.
