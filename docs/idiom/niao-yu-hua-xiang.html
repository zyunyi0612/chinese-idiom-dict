鸟语花香

niǎo yǔ huā xiāng
Literal: "bird, language, flower, fragrance"
Home › Lifestyle › 鸟语花香

📖 Meaning

An idiom describing a scene full of the songs of birds and the scent of flowers. It is used to paint a vivid picture of a beautiful, lively spring landscape. By extension, it conveys a pleasant, harmonious, and idyllic atmosphere.

📜 Origin Story

The phrase traces its roots to classical Chinese literary descriptions of spring scenery rather than a single historical anecdote. It appears in various forms in Tang and Song dynasty poetry and prose celebrating the beauty of nature. The pairing of birdsong and floral fragrance became a stock literary image for an ideal spring day. Over centuries of literary use, the four-character collocation solidified into a fixed idiom.

💡 Example Usage

春天的公园里鸟语花香，吸引了许多市民前来散步赏景。

The spring park was filled with birdsong and the fragrance of flowers, attracting many citizens who came to stroll and enjoy the scenery.
