真情实感

zhēn qíng shí gǎn
Literal: "true, feeling, real, sensation"
Home › Emotion › 真情实感

📖 Meaning

Genuine emotion and authentic feeling. It refers to sincere heartfelt sentiments or real personal experiences, as opposed to artificial or contrived expressions. Often used to describe writing, speech, or art that conveys true emotion.

📜 Origin Story

The expression is documented in modern Chinese literary criticism, notably in Sun Li's essay collection 'Xiulu Ji' (《秀露集》), in the chapter on Ouyang Xiu's prose. Sun Li, a 20th-century Chinese writer of the 'Lotus Creek School' (荷花淀派), argued that prose without concrete constraints and without 真情实感 would ramble boundlessly. It also appears in the poetry of Guo Xiaochuan (郭小川), such as 'Kunlun Xing' (《昆仑行》), where he notes that even ancient myths need not lack genuine feeling. The phrase emphasizes sincerity as the foundation of literary expression.

💡 Example Usage

这篇文章字里行间流露出真情实感，读来令人动容。

This article reveals genuine emotion between the lines, deeply moving the reader.
