抚今悼昔

fǔ jīn dào xī
Literal: "touch, today, mourn, past"
Home › Emotion › 抚今悼昔

📖 Meaning

To reflect on the present and lament the past. It describes being moved by current circumstances to recall and grieve over bygone times. Often conveys a sense of melancholy and nostalgia triggered by a present situation.

📜 Origin Story

The phrase originates from a eulogy titled 'Eulogy for Zhang Shaoyang' by the Ming dynasty scholar Chen Zilong. In the text, Chen writes that reflecting on the present and mourning the past, he utters a sorrowful cry. The expression captures the emotional weight of comparing one's current state with memories of earlier days. It was later adopted by Qing dynasty writers such as Sun Zhiye in his preface to 'An Account of Sorrows in Jiashen,' where he used it to express grief over national turmoil.

💡 Example Usage

重游故地，抚今悼昔，他不禁潸然泪下。

Revisiting the old place, he reflected on the present and mourned the past, and could not help but shed tears.
