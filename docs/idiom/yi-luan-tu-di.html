一乱涂地

yī luàn tú dì
Literal: "one, chaos, smear, ground"
Home › Condition › 一乱涂地

📖 Meaning

Describes a situation that has descended into complete chaos with no way to restore order. The vivid imagery evokes disorder spreading everywhere like mud smeared across the ground. It conveys total, unmanageable collapse.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom originates from the essay 'On Guan Zhong' (管仲论) by Su Xun of the Song Dynasty. Su Xun discusses the statesman Guan Zhong, who served Duke Huan of Qi during the Spring and Autumn period. When Duke Huan died, the state of Qi fell into total chaos because Guan Zhong had already passed away without cultivating a capable successor. Su Xun wrote that upon the duke's death everything collapsed into utter disorder — a situation that was entirely predictable, since the state had relied solely on one man who was already dead.

💡 Example Usage

公司失去核心管理层后，内部一乱涂地，业务全面停摆。

After losing its core management team, the company descended into total chaos, with operations grinding to a complete halt.
