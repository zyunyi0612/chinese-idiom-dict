停辛贮苦

tíng xīn zhù kǔ
Literal: "lingering, hardship, storing, bitterness"
Home › Perseverance › 停辛贮苦

📖 Meaning

To accumulate and endure bitterness and hardship over a long period. It describes someone who has experienced considerable difficulty and suffering. A variant of the idiom 停辛伫苦.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom belongs to the tradition of Chinese four-character expressions describing endurance through adversity. The characters 停 (to linger or remain) and 贮 (to store or accumulate) both convey the sense of hardship persisting and accumulating over time. It is a variant form of 停辛伫苦, with similar meaning and usage. No specific literary origin or associated historical figure is recorded for this particular variant.

💡 Example Usage

父母停辛贮苦一辈子，只为供孩子们读书成才。

Their parents endured a lifetime of hardship, all so their children could study and succeed.
