当风秉烛

dāng fēng bǐng zhú
Literal: "facing the wind, holding, candle"
Home › Condition › 当风秉烛

📖 Meaning

To hold a candle while standing in the wind. It is a metaphor for being in a perilous or precarious situation where one's safety is constantly threatened. The imagery evokes a flame that could be extinguished at any moment.

📜 Origin Story

This idiom originates from the Ming dynasty anonymous play "Kan Jin Huan" (勘金环), Act Three. In the play, a character compares their life to a candle held against the wind, and to a bubble floating on water—both images of extreme fragility. The line vividly conveys the character's sense of imminent danger and helplessness. The phrase has since become a classical metaphor for precarious circumstances.

💡 Example Usage

他的处境恰似当风秉烛，随时都有倾覆的危险。

His situation is like holding a candle in the wind—in danger of collapse at any moment.
