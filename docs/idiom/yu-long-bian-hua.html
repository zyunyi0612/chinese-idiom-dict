鱼龙变化

yú lóng biàn huà
Literal: "fish, dragon, change, transform"
Home › Status › 鱼龙变化

📖 Meaning

Refers to a fish transforming into a dragon. A metaphor for fundamental, profound changes in worldly affairs or in a person's nature and circumstances. It suggests a complete metamorphosis in status, character, or condition.

📜 Origin Story

The idiom originates from a ci poem titled 'Shuilongyin' (Water Dragon Chant) by the Song dynasty poet Liu Kezhuang. The original line reads, 'Let frogs and toads compete for victory, let fish and dragons undergo their transformations—I remain in the mythical land of Huaxu.' The imagery draws on the ancient Chinese legend of carp leaping over the Dragon Gate to become dragons, symbolizing dramatic transformation through effort. Liu Kezhuang contrasts petty worldly rivalries with grand metamorphoses while expressing his own detachment, residing in the dreamlike Huaxu kingdom.

💡 Example Usage

他在异乡打拼多年，鱼龙变化，早已不是当年那个青涩少年。

After years of striving in a foreign land, he has transformed like a fish becoming a dragon, no longer the naive youth he once was.
