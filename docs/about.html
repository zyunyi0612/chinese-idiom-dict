About This Project

What is this?

The Chinese Idiom Dictionary is a free, English-language reference for classical Chinese idioms (成语 / chéngyǔ). Each entry includes:

  • Pinyin with tone marks for correct pronunciation
  • Literal translation of each character
  • English meaning in plain language
  • Origin story — the historical anecdote behind the idiom
  • Example sentences in both Chinese and English
  • Related idioms — similar and opposite expressions

Why Chinese idioms?

Chinese idioms (成语) are four-character expressions that encode thousands of years of history, philosophy, and folk wisdom. Most come from classical literature, historical chronicles, or philosophical texts dating back over 2,000 years.

They are still actively used in everyday Chinese — in conversation, writing, business, and literature. Understanding them is essential for anyone learning Chinese, working with Chinese culture, or studying East Asian history.

Who is this for?

  • Chinese learners at all levels
  • Translators and interpreters working between Chinese and English
  • Writers and readers of Chinese literature
  • Business professionals working with Chinese counterparts
  • Curious minds who enjoy cultural learning

How to use this site

Browse all idioms on the home page, or filter by category to find idioms about a specific theme. Use the search box to look up idioms by Chinese characters, pinyin, or English meaning.
